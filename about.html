Our story

Built to help dancers belong.

CanDance is a Gilbert dance studio where excellent training and genuine encouragement move together.

More than two decades in motion

Where skill meets confidence.

For more than 20 years, CanDance Studios has helped East Valley dancers grow from their first class into confident, expressive performers. The studio was created around a simple belief: young people do their best work when high standards are paired with patient coaching, positive relationships, and room to be themselves.

Today, dancers ages 2–18 learn in our 9,000+ square-foot Gilbert studio. Some come to discover a new hobby. Others train for performances and competitive teams. Every dancer is welcomed as an individual and supported through each stage of their journey.

Our faculty teaches more than choreography. Through consistent training, dancers build discipline, resilience, creativity, teamwork, and friendships that reach far beyond the studio.
